PPU register access map (static)
=================================

The NES PPU is fixed-function: there is no GPU instruction set.
The CPU programs it through these registers. Sites below are
direct absolute accesses in the matching disassembly; SMB also
writes PPUADDR/PPUDATA through the VRAM upload routine at $8E92.

Key routines (from the disassembly):
  $8E2D  clear/load a VRAM block: PPUADDR=$2400, then 768x PPUDATA=$24
         and 64x PPUDATA=$00 (nametable/attribute fill)
  $8E92  VRAM upload interpreter: takes a pointer in $00/$01, sets
         PPUADDR, selects the PPUCTRL increment, and streams bytes to
         PPUDATA with a run-length count
  $8EE6  set PPUADDR high/low from A
  $8EED  write PPUCTRL from A
  $8E5C  read both controllers via the $4016 strobe/shift sequence
  NMI at $8082: writes PPUCTRL/$2001/$2003, OAMDMA from $0200, then
         OAM/scroll/VRAM updates
