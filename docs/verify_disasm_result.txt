## 4. da65 cross-check (secondary, and why it doesn't change the verdict)

I also ran cc65's `da65` (`da65 --cpu 6502 -S 0x8000`) as a second disassembler. It matched 7,940 mnemonics exactly and disagreed on 688. I investigated and every disagreement is a **da65 artifact**, not an smb.asm error:

- da65 linearly decodes data regions as code. In the level data at `$C488+` the bytes `20 40 80` were read as a fake `JSR $8040`, so da65 forced a label at `$8040` and then corrupted its own decode at `$803E` (emitting `.byte $8D / .byte $15`, breaking the real `STA $4015`). smb.asm uses recursive traversal and correctly emits that region as `.byte`.
- For the reported mismatch addresses, the ROM opcode is exactly what smb.asm claims, e.g.:
